Lichess chess opening names
===========================

Source:   lichess-org/chess-openings on GitHub (files a.tsv to e.tsv), fetched 2026-11-27
Used:     the names, ECO codes and moves of 31 openings, in src/games/chess/openings.ts (milestone 15).
          Each line is the list's own line for that name. The one-sentence ideas are Kibitz's own.
