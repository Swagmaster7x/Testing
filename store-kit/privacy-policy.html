Meal & Gym Privacy Policy

Effective 12 November 2026

Meal & Gym is an Android app for planning meals, building a shopping list and logging workouts. It is made by [Your name or business name] ("we"). This policy explains what happens to information when you use the app.

The short version

  • There is no account and no sign-in. Everything you enter stays on your phone, and we never receive it.
  • The free version shows ads from Google AdMob. Google collects some information from your device to show and measure those ads, as described below.
  • If you buy "Remove ads", Google Play handles the payment. We never see your payment details.

What you enter stays on your phone

Names you give each person, meal plans, recipes, shopping lists, workouts, weights and reps, sauna times and settings are stored only on your device. We do not run a server, and the app does not send this information to us or to anyone else.

Some things leave your phone only when you choose: exporting a backup or a meal plan writes a file to a place you pick, and sharing a shopping list sends it to the app you pick. What happens to it then is up to you and that app.

Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage in Android settings, deletes everything it stored. Backup files you exported are not affected.

Ads (free version)

The free version shows banner ads from Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC. To show, limit and measure ads and to prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK in the app collects and shares with Google:

  • Device identifiers, including your device's advertising ID
  • Your IP address, which can be used to estimate your general location
  • Interactions with ads, such as views and taps
  • Diagnostic information, such as app performance and crash data related to ads

This information is sent encrypted. Google's use of it is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Your choices. If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the app asks for your consent before any ads are requested, using Google's consent tool, and you can change your choice at any time in Settings > Privacy choices. Anywhere, you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your phone's Settings under Privacy > Ads (the exact menu varies by phone). Buying "Remove ads" stops ads straight away, and from the next launch the ads SDK is not started at all.

Purchases

"Remove ads" is a one-time purchase handled by Google Play under Google Payments' privacy notice. The app only asks Google Play whether the purchase was made, so it can turn ads off. We do not receive your name, email or payment details.

Health and fitness information

The app is a planner. It does not record body weight, calorie totals or health conditions, and it does not connect to Health Connect or other health services. The meal and workout information you enter stays on your phone, as described above. Nutrition text on recipes is approximate reference information. The app is not medical, nutrition or fitness advice.

Children

Meal & Gym is meant for adults and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Permissions

  • Notifications, to show the sauna timer while it runs and alert you when it ends.
  • Internet and network state, used by the ads SDK and Google Play billing only.
  • Vibrate, wake lock and a foreground service, so the sauna and rest timers keep time and buzz with the screen off.
  • Advertising ID, used by the ads SDK as described above.
